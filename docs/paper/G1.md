## Read-out, fixed before the pilot is scored

For each dev dataset on ResNet-50, report the mean paired difference in CD@5% (CDEA minus baseline), averaged over seeds 0 and 1, with a 95% bootstrap interval over images. Report the drops in z_k and z_l separately beside it.

Margin attribution has a Grad-CAM and an IG variant in the grid. The read-out compares CDEA with the better of the two on each dataset, so the skeptic baseline is not under-tuned. Contrastive Extremal Perturbations is read at a = 5%.

- If CDEA is ahead of margin attribution on both dev datasets (mean difference above 0), the paper is the method paper. Seeds 2–4 training and val selection continue.
- The deletion Extremal Perturbations comparator is read the same way. The stop rule: if CDEA is ahead of both the better margin variant and deletion Extremal Perturbations on both dev datasets, continue as a method paper. Otherwise take the evaluation-paper route and do not revise the formulation again. Log the decision in `PROGRESS.md`.

Two datasets cannot support a significance test. The read-out is a decision aid on val. It is not a paper result, and none of its numbers appear in the paper.
