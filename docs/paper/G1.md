# Gate G1

G1 is the framing decision. It decides which headline claim the paper makes. It does not gate writing, and it does not wait for the G0 reproductions.

User decision, 2026-10-07.

## What it decides

- **Method paper.** CDEA's unique evidence is more contrastive than margin attribution, contrastive Extremal Perturbations, and CVE at matched area (claim C1 in `EVAL_PLAN.md`).
- **Evaluation paper.** The contribution is the contrastive evaluation protocol, and the finding is that margin attribution matches or beats mask optimisation under it, together with the lesion-overlap negative result.

## What it does not block

- Writing the problem formulation, method, evaluation protocol, related work, and the appendices that describe them.
- The G0 reproductions.
- Val selection runs. They read the same val cells as the pilot.

## Pilot

Family C, CD@5% under ROAD, val only. Datasets: CIFAR-10 and HAM10000. Backbones: ResNet-50, with ViT-B/16 reported beside it. Seeds 0 and 1. n = 64 per cell. The CDEA configuration is the runner default, not a val-selected configuration.

Comparisons:

- CDEA vs margin attribution, on CD@5%;
- CDEA vs contrastive Extremal Perturbations, on CD@5%;
- CDEA vs CVE, on CD1@5%, ResNet-50 only.

## Read-out, fixed before the pilot is scored

For each dev dataset on ResNet-50, report the mean paired difference in CD@5% (CDEA minus baseline), averaged over seeds 0 and 1, with a 95% bootstrap interval over images. Report the drops in z_k and z_l separately beside it.

Margin attribution has a Grad-CAM and an IG variant in the grid. The read-out compares CDEA with the better of the two on each dataset, so the skeptic baseline is not under-tuned. Contrastive Extremal Perturbations is read at a = 5%.

- If CDEA is ahead of margin attribution on both dev datasets (mean difference above 0), the paper is the method paper. Seeds 2–4 training and val selection continue.
- The deletion Extremal Perturbations comparator is read the same way. The stop rule: if CDEA is ahead of both the better margin variant and deletion Extremal Perturbations on both dev datasets, continue as a method paper. Otherwise take the evaluation-paper route and do not revise the formulation again. Log the decision in `PROGRESS.md`.

Two datasets cannot support a significance test. The read-out is a decision aid on val. It is not a paper result, and none of its numbers appear in the paper.

## Declared departure, 2026-10-11

**Seed-0 read.** CDEA was behind margin Grad-CAM on CD@5% under ROAD on both dev datasets (val, ResNet-50, n = 64). Under the stop rule above, that points to the evaluation-paper route.

**Diagnosis.** It was not an implementation bug. The optimised payoff rises, and CDEA's own hard cells beat margin Grad-CAM's on the same cells. The advantage is lost when the boundary of the mask moves to the scored mask (`FORMULATION.md` section 4.1).

**Decision.** By user decision, one formulation change is tested before the stop rule applies: the boundary-robust payoff of `FORMULATION.md` section 4.1. Its design is fixed in that section before any of its numbers exist. It has no tuned value.

**Read-out, fixed before it runs.**
- **Data.** Seed 0, val, ResNet-50, n = 64, CIFAR-10 and HAM10000. It uses the same images, checkpoints and baseline rows as the seed-0 read.
- **Comparison.** For each dataset, the mean paired difference in CD@5% under ROAD (CDEA with the boundary-robust payoff, minus the comparator), with a 95% bootstrap interval over images.
- **Comparators.** Margin Grad-CAM (the better margin variant on both datasets in the seed-0 read) and deletion Extremal Perturbations.
- **Reported beside it.**
  - CDEA with the fixed-boundary payoff, on the same images.
  - D2, and the hard-to-scored payoff gap (`scripts/check_degenerate.py --records`).
  - The drops in z_k and z_l.

**Decision rule.**
- **Adopt** if the mean difference is above 0 against both comparators on both datasets. Section 4.1 becomes the method, and the G1 read is repeated on seed 1 when it is trained.
- **Otherwise,** take the evaluation-paper route, with no further formulation change. Section 4.1 is reported as ablation A10.

Either way, log the outcome in `PROGRESS.md`.

**Outcome, 2026-10-11.** The rule was met, and it was met again with offset seeds 1 and 2. The user adopted section 4.1 as the method.

Still open:
- the seed-1 repeat (seed 1 is not trained);
- D2 at 5% on CIFAR-10, which fails for every offset seed.

**After the D2 fix, 2026-10-11.** Binary draws (`FORMULATION.md` section 7) close D2 at every area on both dev datasets. With them, the same read-out is not met. CDEA is behind margin Grad-CAM on both datasets, and still ahead of deletion Extremal Perturbations:

`scripts/g1_readout.py --records results/paper/cells results/paper/cdea_offset_seeds results/paper/cdea_binary --cdea cdea cdea:A10_boundary_shift --seeds 0`

The earlier pass therefore depended on the fractional plan that D2 rejects. Under the stop rule this points to the evaluation-paper route. The decision is the user's.

**Decision, 2026-10-11.** The user chose the evaluation-paper route. There is no further formulation change. CDEA stays in the evaluation as the mask-optimisation method, in its current form: the boundary-robust payoff with binary draws. Its fractional and fixed-boundary forms are ablations A12 and A10.

## Reopened, 2026-10-13: fractional-mask test

**Held, 2026-10-14 (user decision, never launched).** The 2026-10-13 optimisation diagnostic put A12 level with margin Grad-CAM on CD (−0.02 and −0.19), so the test would likely spend seed 1 on a predictable fail. The method question moved to the decomposition game (`FORMULATION_DECOMPOSITION.md`, branch `feat/cdea-decomposition`). The section below is kept as written.

**Why.** A seed-0 diagnostic ran after the 2026-10-11 decision (val, ResNet-50, n = 48, CD@5% under ROAD, CIFAR-10 and ImageNet). It removed one ingredient of CDEA at a time. Only fractional masks (A12) came level with margin Grad-CAM:
- **A12 minus margin Grad-CAM:** ImageNet +0.08 [−0.49, +0.63]. CIFAR-10 −0.27 under ROAD and +0.25 under blur.
- **CDEA (binary draws) minus margin Grad-CAM:** CIFAR-10 −0.24. ImageNet −0.69 [−1.23, −0.17].

By user decision, the method question is reopened for this one test. It is the second departure from the stop rule. The arm was chosen after reading seed-0 val, so the test runs on data that no CDEA of this formulation has seen. The rule below is fixed before any of that data is scored. From the seed-0 numbers, the expected difference is near 0.

**Data.**
- Seed 1, val, ResNet-50, n = 200 per cell, all 8 contrastive datasets.
- Models: the seed-1 checkpoints of the 7 trained probes, and the torchvision weights on ImageNet.
- Images: the seed-1 sample draw (generator seed 1001, `EVAL_PLAN.md` section 2.4).
- No CDEA of this formulation has run on seed 1, and nothing about fractional against binary masks was chosen on it.
- **Disclosed.**
  - `results/paper/runs/val/contrastive/*/*/seed1` holds earlier-formulation cells from 2026-10-10. They are n = 64: the first 64 images of this draw, with baseline rows from older code. No document records that they were read.
  - On ImageNet, seed 1 uses the same weights as seed 0 and differs only in its images. About 4 of its 200 images are expected to overlap seed 0's.
  - A fast-knob smoke cell (brain tumor, one image) checked that seed 1 loads its own checkpoint. Its numbers were not read, and it was deleted.
- The test split is not read.

**Arms.** Full knobs, one method code hash for every row, recorded at launch. Nothing is tuned.
- **Fractional:** `cdea:A12_fractional_masks`. This is the runner default with `binary_masks=False`: the boundary-robust payoff (`FORMULATION.md` section 4.1), Grad-CAM initialisation, T = 100, η = 0.1, draw seed 0.
- **Binary:** `cdea`, the current method, on the same images.

**Comparators.** As in the stop rule:
- the better margin variant on each dataset: margin Grad-CAM or margin IG, whichever has the higher mean CD on these images, chosen once on the full sample;
- deletion Extremal Perturbations (`extremal`).

**Measure.** CD@5% under ROAD, paired per image.
- **Per dataset:** the mean paired difference (arm minus comparator), with a 95% bootstrap interval over images.
- **Pooled:** the unweighted mean of the 8 per-dataset differences, with a 95% interval from a stratified bootstrap (images resampled within each dataset, 10,000 draws, seed 0).

**An arm passes when all four hold:**
1. The pooled interval against the better margin variant is above 0.
2. The pooled interval against deletion Extremal Perturbations is above 0.
3. On no dataset is the interval against the better margin variant entirely below 0.
4. The arm and both comparators are complete on all 8 datasets.

This is stricter than the "mean above 0 on both dev datasets" read used earlier. That read chose between two routes. This one decides whether a method claim comes back after two misses, with an expected difference near 0, where a sign rule is a coin flip.

**Decision.**
- **The binary arm passes:** method paper, with CDEA as it is. Fractional masks stay ablation A12. The binary arm runs anyway as the reference, and a pass on seed 1 would make the seed-0 read the outlier, which the full grid then reports.
- **Only the fractional arm passes:** method paper, with fractional masks. `FORMULATION.md` section 7 changes, binary draws become an ablation, and D2 is handled as below.
- **Neither passes:** the evaluation-paper route stands, and the method question is closed. No further formulation is tested.

**D2 if fractional masks are adopted (user sign-off needed before launch).** D2 failed for fractional plans on seed-0 CIFAR-10 at 5%. The proposal:
- D2 becomes reported, not a gate, for CDEA. C1 is scored on the top-a mask, which D2 does not change. D2 guards claims about the optimised payoff, and the paper then makes none.
- The paper reports the soft-minus-hard gap per dataset and says that the optimised payoff overstates what the scored mask achieves.
- The alternative keeps D2 as a gate. A fractional pass with D2 failing on any dataset then still means the evaluation-paper route.

**Reported beside, with no role in the decision:**
- CD@5% under blur;
- the four logit drops z_k|M_k, z_l|M_k, z_k|M_l, z_l|M_l (the evidence leak);
- CVE on CD1;
- per arm and dataset: D2 and the hard-minus-scored gap.

ViT-B/16 is not part of this test. If fractional masks are adopted, ViT-B/16 runs in the normal grid as the robustness check.

**Procedure.**
1. Commit this section and `scripts/fractional_readout.py` before launch. Log the commit and the method code hash in `PROGRESS.md`.
2. For each of the 8 datasets, run:

   `PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/paper_run.py --game contrastive --dataset <dataset> --backbone resnet50 --seed 1 --split val --n 200 --methods cdea,base_evidence,margin_gradcam,margin_ig,extremal,cve,random_floor --ablations A12_fractional_masks --areas 0.05 --operators road,blur --out results/paper/fractional_test`

3. No record is read until all 8 cells have exited. A failed cell is rerun once, unchanged. If it fails again, that dataset is missing, and rule 4 applies.
4. Read out with:

   `PYTHONPATH=. python scripts/fractional_readout.py --records results/paper/fractional_test --expect-hash <hash>`

   The script prints the decision. Log the outcome in `PROGRESS.md`.

**Cost.** From the seed-0 timings at 5% only, a cell takes about 6 minutes per CDEA arm and about 55 minutes for deletion Extremal Perturbations. That is about 1.2 GPU-hours per cell and about 10 for the test.
